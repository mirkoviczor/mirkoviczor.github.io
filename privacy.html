Privacy Policy – Bookkeeping AI Sync

Bookkeeping AI Sync is a private application used to automate bookkeeping workflows.

Google Drive access is used to retrieve financial documents and upload completed reports to a privately managed Ubuntu server and Google Drive.

Selected documents may be processed using OpenAI's AI services to perform bookkeeping analysis.

We do not sell personal information or use client documents for advertising.

Access is restricted to authorized users. Google Drive access can be revoked through Google Account security settings.
